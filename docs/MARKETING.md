# Green Utility Log — Marketing Starter Kit

**Stage:** VeChain **testnet** beta · **Goal:** recruit a small, engaged group of beta testers
**Audience:** global, English-speaking VeChain / VeBetterDAO community
**Golden rule:** be honest. It's testnet. Test-B3TR has **no monetary value**. You're
recruiting people to *test and shape* the app — not to "earn free money". Honesty
attracts the right testers and keeps farmers and disappointed users away.

## 1. Positioning — what makes it worth testing

- **Real-world action, on-chain proof.** Not a tap-to-earn game — you log an actual
  utility meter. Each reading becomes a VeBetterDAO proof-of-impact.
- **Honest impact.** You're rewarded for *using less* than your own recent average
  (conservation), with the CO₂ saving recorded in the proof — not just for logging.
- **Anti-farming by design.** One photo can only ever earn once (server-side hash
  dedupe), wallet-ownership proof, per-meter cooldown. Worth saying out loud — it
  signals you built it seriously.
- **Mobile-first, no install.** Opens in the browser, connect VeWorld, done.

Currently live: **electricity only** (gas/water/solar come later — say this, it sets
expectations and gives you a "what's next" story).

## 4. The new-tester onboarding flow (make this frictionless)

A tester's path must be 5 minutes or you lose them:

1. Sees your tweet/Telegram → clicks app link
2. Installs VeWorld, **switches to testnet** ← biggest drop-off point; spell it out
3. Gets free VTHO from the faucet (linked in-app Help) ← second drop-off point
4. Connects wallet, registers electricity meter, snaps photo, submits
5. Gets test-B3TR → posts a screenshot in Telegram 🎉

**Reduce friction:** a 30–60s screen-recording of the full flow (you doing it on your
phone) is the single highest-converting asset you can make. Pin it in Telegram and post
it on X. People copy what they can see.
